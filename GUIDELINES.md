# Landing-page guidelines (distilled from web research, 2026-07-02)

Sources: Evil Martians "We studied 100 devtool landing pages" + "How to kill conversions
on your developer tool landing page"; SaaS Hero B2B SaaS landing-page best practices;
iStudiosMedia visual-hierarchy data; everything.design B2B messaging guide.

1. **5-second test.** What it is, who it's for, next step — all above the fold.
   ~57% of viewing time is spent there.
2. **Headline ≤8 words, outcome not feature.**
3. **Show the product in the hero** (demo, animation, real output) — don't describe it.
4. **Credibility block immediately after the hero** — client logos, testimonials, or
   founder credentials. Fastest trust builder on devtool pages.
5. **One primary CTA, repeated, zero friction.** Direct booking beat enterprise forms
   2% vs 0.1%. A dead/placeholder button is the worst possible state.
6. **Value-framed CTA copy** ("See your board on the bench") beats time/cost-framed
   ("Book 15 minutes") by ~2x in benchmark tests.
7. **Specificity over adjectives** for technical buyers: numbers, run IDs, protocols,
   "lowest possible abstraction". No "blazing fast", no "leading companies trust us".
8. **Speak to every deal stakeholder** (avg 4-6 in B2B): engineer gets proof, manager
   gets ROI, exec gets outcome. One line each is enough on a startup page.
9. **One artifact told once, deeply.** Don't repeat the same proof across sections.
10. **Compatibility strip** — supported MCUs/buses/toolchains as a scannable block;
    signals maturity.
11. **Objections/FAQ** — top 3 pushbacks, one-line answers.
12. **Never done.** Measure, test with real target users, iterate on data not opinions.
